# Briefing Note Prompt

I work for a public organization. Our executive leadership team is scheduled to meet with [Company Name].

Please:

Review the attached files for context.

Conduct a comprehensive review of all relevant content on: [Company Website URL].

Perform broad external research on [Company Name], including products, services, reputation, partnerships, financial context (if public), and recent news.

Based on all findings, draft a clear, concise, and accurate executive briefing note suitable for senior leadership review.

Use the structure and formatting from the attached template titled ["Briefing Note template file name"].

If any information is missing or unclear, ask follow-up questions to ensure the briefing note is complete and decision-ready.
